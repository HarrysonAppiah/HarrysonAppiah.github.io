I grieve a society that has perfected the art of selling necessities back to the people who need them.

A roof over one’s head, once a refuge, has become a financial instrument dressed in the language of aspiration.

At nearly 7% interest, the house you buy may outlive the years you spend paying for it.

The bank owns the interest, the market dictates the price, and the worker mortgages tomorrow to afford today.

Even the earth beneath our feet seems to belong to whoever can extract the highest return from it.

We call it ownership, yet spend decades negotiating our freedom with institutions that profit from our need for shelter.

> **Perhaps the cruelest trick of modern civilization is convincing us that lifelong indebtedness is the price of dignity.**
